Tender poached leeks lined up on an oval bistro platter on a linen tablecloth, glistening under a mustardy shallot vinaigrette, generously topped with grated hard-boiled egg mimosa and snipped chives, vintage fork resting on the platter's edge.

Fresh leeks being trimmed on a wooden cutting board, green tops split lengthwise in a cross, one leek being rinsed fanned-open under cold running water over a sink, grit washing away.

Trimmed leeks simmering in a large pot of boiling salted water, steam rising, a knife tip testing the tenderness of one leek held with tongs, kitchen timer beside the stove.

## step-3.webp
Eggs hard-boiling in a small saucepan of bubbling water beside the leek pot, one cooked egg being cooled under cold water in someone's hand, another peeled smooth on a small plate.

## step-4.webp
Poached leeks laid out flat to drain on a clean striped tea towel, glistening and tender, gentle steam rising as they cool, kitchen counter in soft daylight.

## step-5.webp
A vinaigrette being made in a small bowl: mustard and wine vinegar whisked together, oil drizzling in from a spout to emulsify into a creamy dressing, finely chopped shallot on a board beside a small whisk.

## step-6.webp
The warm drained leeks arranged neatly on an oval serving platter, a spoon napping them generously with the pale mustard vinaigrette, the dressing pooling between the leeks.

## step-7.webp
Hard-boiled egg being grated on the coarse side of a box grater over the dressed leeks, a fine yellow-and-white mimosa snowfall settling on top, snipped chives scattered from fingers, ready to serve.
