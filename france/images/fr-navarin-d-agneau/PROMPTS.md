## hero.webp
A rustic Dutch oven of navarin d'agneau: tender chunks of braised lamb shoulder in a light glossy tomato-tinted sauce with carrot chunks, turnip quarters, halved potatoes, pearl onions and bright green garden peas, steam rising, served at the table on a linen tablecloth with slices of country bread beside.

## step-1.webp
Chunks of lamb shoulder, cut into 5 cm pieces and seasoned, searing in olive oil in an enameled cast-iron Dutch oven, deep golden crust forming, tongs turning one piece, light shimmer of hot oil.

## step-2.webp
Flour being sprinkled from a spoon over the browned lamb pieces in the pot, a wooden spoon stirring to coat them, a spoonful of tomato paste and crushed garlic cloves waiting on a small plate beside the stove.

## step-3.webp
Hot chicken stock being poured from a saucepan into the Dutch oven over the floured lamb, liquid rising to cover the meat, a bouquet garni of thyme, bay leaf and parsley tied with string resting on top.

## step-4.webp
Spring vegetables being prepared on a wooden cutting board: carrots cut into chunks, turnips into quarters, halved small potatoes and peeled pearl onions in neat piles, a paring knife and vegetable peelings beside.

## step-5.webp
The prepared carrots, turnips, potatoes and pearl onions being added into the simmering pot of lamb, vegetables settling into the light brown sauce, wooden spoon tucking them under the surface.

## step-6.webp
Bright green garden peas being poured from a bowl into the uncovered pot of navarin, sauce reduced and lightly coating a spoon held above the stew, vegetables and lamb glistening.

## step-7.webp
The spent bouquet garni being lifted out of the finished navarin with tongs, a small spoon tasting the sauce, salt and pepper mills beside the pot on the counter.

## step-8.webp
The finished navarin d'agneau served at the table in its Dutch oven, lid set aside, portions being ladled into shallow bowls, slices of country bread and a linen napkin beside, steam rising.
