## hero.webp
A platter of œufs mimosa on a linen tablecloth: halved hard-boiled egg whites generously filled with pale golden yolk-and-mayonnaise cream piped in rosettes, dusted with finely grated egg yolk like mimosa blossom and scattered with snipped chives, bistro cutlery and a small glass of white wine blurred behind.

## step-1.webp
Six eggs sitting in a saucepan of water coming to a gentle boil on the stovetop, small bubbles rising around the shells, a kitchen timer beside the pan.

## step-2.webp
Hard-boiled eggs being plunged into a glass bowl of iced water with ice cubes floating, one egg being peeled with the shell coming away cleanly, shells collected on a small plate.

## step-3.webp
Homemade mayonnaise being whisked in a ceramic bowl: thick glossy pale-yellow emulsion clinging to the whisk, sunflower oil being poured in a thin stream from a bottle, a spoonful of mustard and half a lemon beside the bowl.

## step-4.webp
Peeled hard-boiled eggs halved lengthways on a wooden cutting board, firm yolks being gently scooped out with a teaspoon into a small bowl, empty white halves lined up neatly.

## step-5.webp
Cooked egg yolks being mashed with a fork and folded into the bowl of homemade mayonnaise, smooth pale golden cream forming, one whole yolk set aside on a saucer, pepper mill nearby.

## step-6.webp
Egg white halves being filled with the yolk-mayonnaise cream using a fluted piping bag, generous swirled rosettes forming, filled and unfilled halves side by side on a serving platter.

## step-7.webp
A hard-boiled egg yolk being grated on a fine grater over the filled eggs, fine yellow crumbs falling like mimosa blossom, snipped chives scattered over the platter, ready to serve.
