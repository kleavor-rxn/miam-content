## hero.webp
A rustic earthenware dish of ratatouille: glossy stewed eggplant cubes, zucchini, red and yellow pepper strips and tomato quarters in a rich olive-oil-slicked jus, thyme sprig and bay leaf on top, vivid Provençal colors, wooden spoon in the dish on a linen tablecloth, summer light.

## step-1.webp
A wooden cutting board covered with neatly prepped vegetables: diced eggplant, diced zucchini, red and yellow pepper strips, quartered tomatoes and sliced onions, chef's knife resting across the board.

## step-2.webp
Eggplant cubes browning in shimmering olive oil in a large sauté pan, edges caramelized golden brown, wooden spatula tossing them, bottle of olive oil blurred behind.

## step-3.webp
Zucchini cubes turning golden in the same sauté pan with a fresh drizzle of olive oil, the browned eggplant waiting in a bowl beside the stove.

## step-4.webp
Sliced onions and strips of red and yellow pepper softening slowly in olive oil in the sauté pan, translucent and glossy but not browned, wooden spoon stirring.

## step-5.webp
Quartered tomatoes, crushed garlic cloves, thyme sprigs and a bay leaf being added on top of the softened onions and peppers in the pan, salt falling from a pinch of fingers.

## step-6.webp
All the vegetables reunited in the covered sauté pan, gently stewing in their ruby-red tomato juices over low heat, lid lifted to show a stir with a wooden spoon, steam rising.

## step-7.webp
The finished ratatouille simmering uncovered, juices reduced and glossy, vegetables soft and jewel-colored, a spoon lifting a taste above the pan.
