## hero.webp
Six small round Mauritian sandwich biscuits, each about 6 cm across and 1.5 cm tall, arranged on a plain white plate. Each one is made of two pale, barely coloured, crumbly butter shortbread discs stacked on top of each other, with a thin line of red strawberry jam visible between them. The top of each biscuit is covered with a smooth, opaque, candy-pink icing that has set with a soft sheen, running a little way down the sides in gentle drips. One biscuit is cut in half to show the pale, short, sandy layers and the red jam in the middle.
