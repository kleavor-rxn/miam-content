Every ball is completely and evenly covered in short, thin, dark brown chocolate sprinkles and sits in its own small plain brown pleated paper candy cup. Two brigadeiros are cut in half and lean open against their neighbours, showing a very smooth, dense, glossy, fudgy dark chocolate interior with a soft sticky shine, like thick chocolate caramel. The overall look is homemade and shiny, like a birthday party sweet, photographed from a gentle three-quarter angle in soft window light.
