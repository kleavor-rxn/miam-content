Six square sponge cakes, each about 5 cm across and 4 cm tall, arranged on a plain white plate, with one cake cut in half to show the inside. Each cube is completely coated in a thin, dark brown chocolate icing that has soaked slightly into the edges, then covered all over with fine, dry, white desiccated coconut, so the outside looks white and shaggy with brown showing through. The cut cake reveals a light, airy, pale yellow sponge framed by a thin dark chocolate border just under the coconut. A few loose flakes of coconut are scattered on the plate.
