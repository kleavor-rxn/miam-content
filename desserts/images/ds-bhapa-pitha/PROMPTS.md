## hero.webp
Three small steamed rice cakes on a plain white plate, each a soft, rounded dome about 8 cm across and 4 cm high, matte snow-white with a fine, slightly grainy, powdery-soft surface. One cake is broken open in the middle to reveal a moist, dark brown filling of melted palm jaggery mixed with flecks of white grated coconut, oozing slightly onto the plate. Thin squares of damp white muslin lie under the cakes. The light catches the delicate crumbly texture of the rice flour.
