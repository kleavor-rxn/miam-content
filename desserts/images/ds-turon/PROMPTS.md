Five crisp fried banana rolls, each about 12 cm long and 3 cm thick, cylindrical with tapered folded ends, stacked loosely on a plain white plate. The thin spring-roll wrappers are golden brown, blistered and crackly, coated in a shiny, hardened amber-brown caramel glaze with darker caramelised spots and a few drips of set sugar on the plate. One roll is cut in half crosswise, showing a soft, pale yellow cooked banana inside and a thin strip of orange-yellow jackfruit.
