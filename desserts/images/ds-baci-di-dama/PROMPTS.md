About a dozen small round sandwich biscuits on a plain white plate, each about 3 cm across, made of two pale golden hazelnut shortbread domes joined flat sides together by a thin visible ring of glossy dark chocolate. The domes have a slightly crackled, sandy matte surface with tiny specks of ground hazelnut. One biscuit lies on its side to show the two halves and the chocolate in between, and another is split open showing the chocolate on its flat side. A few whole toasted, skinned hazelnuts are scattered beside the plate.
