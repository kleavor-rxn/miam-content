A tall plain white round bowl with straight smooth sides, seen from a low three-quarter angle, filled to the brim with a loose, uneven, fluffy heap of finely shaved white ice made of soft snowy flakes. Many short, thin, wiggly, bright emerald-green pandan jelly noodles, about 3 cm long and 3 mm thick and shaped like little tadpole tails, are tangled all through the ice and pile up thickly on top of it. A thick, glossy, dark amber palm sugar syrup is poured over the top and runs down the sides in a few streaks, and creamy white coconut milk pools between the ice flakes. A small spoonful of soft dark red sweet beans sits at the foot of the heap, and a plain metal spoon rests against the rim.
