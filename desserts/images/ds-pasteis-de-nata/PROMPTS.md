## hero.webp
Four small round Portuguese custard tarts, each about 7 cm across and deep, standing on a plain white plate. Each tart has a tall, crisp, deeply laminated puff pastry shell with many fine horizontal flaky layers visible all around the sides, rising straight up with a rough, slightly uneven rim. The shell is filled to the brim with a smooth, glossy, pale yellow egg custard whose surface is flat and blistered with several large, irregular, dark brown to almost black scorched patches. One tart is dusted with a little ground cinnamon, and a small plain white bowl of ground cinnamon sits beside the plate.
