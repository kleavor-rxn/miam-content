## hero.webp
Six small rectangular no-bake bars, each about 5 by 3.5 cm and 3 cm tall, arranged on a plain white plate, one turned on its side to show its layers clearly. Each bar has three distinct, neat layers: a dark brown, crumbly, slightly coarse base of chocolate biscuit crumbs flecked with coconut and chopped almond, a thick smooth middle layer of pale yellow buttercream, and a thin, glossy, firm layer of dark chocolate on top. The cut sides are sharp and clean, so the three stripes of dark brown, pale yellow and very dark brown are clearly visible.
