Two small cone-shaped frozen milk desserts, each about eight centimetres tall, lying unmoulded side by side on a plain white plate, with a flat wide base and a rounded tip. Their colour is a creamy ivory tinged with pale beige from the reduced milk, with tiny visible flecks of green pistachio and dark cardamom throughout, and a dense, smooth, slightly frosted surface.
