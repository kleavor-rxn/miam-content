## hero.webp
Several round Swedish cinnamon buns, each about 8 cm across, sitting in plain white pleated paper baking cases on a light wooden board. Each bun is a tight spiral of soft enriched dough baked glossy golden brown, with thin dark brown swirls of buttery cinnamon filling visible between the coils. The tops are scattered generously with coarse, opaque white pearl sugar nibs that do not melt. One bun is pulled slightly open to show the soft, fluffy, pale crumb and the cinnamon swirl inside.
