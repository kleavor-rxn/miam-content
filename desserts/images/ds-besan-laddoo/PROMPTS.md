They are a warm golden-brown to toasted ochre, with a rough, coarse, sandy, crumbly surface of tiny visible grains of roasted flour and a few pressed-in slivers of pistachio and almond. Their sides have small cracks and bumps from being pressed by hand, and a faint sheen of ghee. One sweet in front is broken in half, revealing a dense, sandy, crumbly, golden-brown interior of fine grains. The plate sits on a bare, smooth, plain pale wooden tabletop that is completely empty and uniform all around it, lit by soft daylight from the side.
