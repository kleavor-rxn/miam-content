## hero.webp
Four round Japanese daifuku mochi, each about 6 cm across and gently domed like soft white pillows, on a small plain pale ceramic plate. The outer skin is smooth, matt, soft white glutinous rice dough lightly dusted with fine white starch. One daifuku is cut cleanly in half to show a thin, stretchy white rice layer wrapped around a dense, smooth, dark purplish-brown sweet red bean paste filling. The other three are whole, soft and slightly irregular, as if shaped by hand.
