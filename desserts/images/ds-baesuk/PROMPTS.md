A plain white shallow porcelain bowl seen from above, filled with clear, pale golden-amber ginger and honey liquid, served cold. Four thick crescent-shaped wedges of peeled Asian pear lie in the liquid, each resting on its flat cut side so that its smooth rounded back faces up, the ivory flesh slightly translucent. Pressed into the rounded back of every wedge are three tiny, perfectly round, matte black peppercorns in a short row, each sitting half-buried like a small black bead, with no seeds or pips showing anywhere on the flesh. A few small pale pine nuts rest on the surface of the liquid between the wedges. The bowl fills the frame and the background is plain pale wood.
