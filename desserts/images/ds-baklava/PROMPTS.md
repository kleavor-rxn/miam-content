Five diamond-shaped pieces of pistachio baklava, each about 5 cm long, arranged on a plain white plate. The top is many paper-thin, crisp, flaky sheets of pastry baked a deep golden brown and glistening with clear sugar syrup. The cut sides show a thick, vivid bright green layer of finely ground pistachio sandwiched between stacked golden pastry layers. A light sprinkle of fine green pistachio powder sits on top of each piece, and one piece lies on its side in front to show the layers.
