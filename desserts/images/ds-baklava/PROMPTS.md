## hero.webp
Five diamond-shaped pieces of pistachio baklava, each about 5 cm long, arranged on a plain white plate, photographed from a three-quarter angle. The top of every piece is made of many thin, dry, crisp sheets of filo pastry stacked flat and baked deep golden brown, with visible ragged edges and tiny blisters, lightly glistening with clear sugar syrup. The cut sides show eight to ten fine golden pastry layers with a thick vivid bright green layer of coarsely ground pistachio in the middle. A small pinch of fine green pistachio crumbs sits on top of each piece, and one piece lies on its side in front to show the layers.
