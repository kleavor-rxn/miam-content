Six small round open tarts, each about 10 cm across, arranged on a plain white plate and seen slightly from above. Each tart has a thin, pale golden shortcrust case whose raised rim, about 1.5 cm high, has been pinched all the way round into a fluted, crown-like edge. The cases are filled level with a moist, loose, glossy golden-brown grated coconut filling, its shreds clearly visible, with darker caramelised tips. One tart sits slightly apart in the foreground, with a few pastry crumbs beside it.
