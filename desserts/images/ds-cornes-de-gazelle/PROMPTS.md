## hero.webp
About a dozen Moroccan cornes de gazelle, each roughly 7 cm long, slim curved crescent horns plump in the middle and tapering to fine pointed tips, piled loosely on a plain round white plate. The paper-thin baked pastry is perfectly smooth, matt and pale ivory with a faint golden blush, dusted lightly with icing sugar, and every horn has a neat, evenly curved outline. One horn is broken in half, showing a dense, moist, pale golden-beige ground-almond paste filling inside the thin crust. A small plain clear glass of amber mint tea with a sprig of fresh mint stands beside the plate.
