Three round apples standing upright on a plain white plate, each with a plain wooden stick pushed straight down into its top. Each apple is entirely covered from stalk to base in one thick, smooth, glossy amber-brown caramel shell, so no green skin is visible anywhere; the caramel is dark toffee brown, drips slightly and gathers in a small flat pool at the base. One apple has its lower half rolled in chopped roasted peanuts, another has thin drizzled lines of dark chocolate across the caramel, and the third is plain glossy caramel.
