## hero.webp
Five round, softly domed Mexican sweet bread rolls about 10 cm across, arranged on a plain white platter. Each roll is a golden-brown bread bun whose whole upper half is covered by a thin, crumbly, sandy, cracked layer of sugar paste, pressed with three or four curved parallel grooves that sweep across the top like the ridges of a scallop shell; three crusts are pale cream-white and two are cocoa brown. The sugar layer is dry and powdery with fine cracks, and the golden bread rises gently beneath its edge. One roll in front is torn in half, revealing a fluffy, pale yellow, fine-textured crumb.
