Five round, domed sweet buns about 10 cm across, arranged on a plain white platter. Each bun is capped with a thick, matt, slightly cracked sugar crust scored with deep curved grooves that fan out like a scallop shell; three crusts are pale cream-white and two are cocoa brown. Around the edge of each crust the soft, golden-brown bread shows through. One bun in front is torn in half, revealing a fluffy, pale yellow, fine-textured crumb.
