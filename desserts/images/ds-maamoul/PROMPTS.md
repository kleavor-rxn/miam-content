Several small round domed semolina pastries, each about 5 cm across and 3 cm tall, arranged on a plain white plate. Each pastry has a pale golden-beige surface pressed with an intricate raised pattern of fine radiating ridges and grooves, and a flat, lightly golden base. They are generously dusted with a thick, snowy layer of icing sugar that settles in the grooves of the pattern. One pastry is broken in half, showing a crumbly, sandy, pale yellow semolina shell around a moist filling of coarsely chopped walnuts.
