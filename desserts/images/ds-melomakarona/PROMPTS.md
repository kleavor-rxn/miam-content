## hero.webp
About a dozen plain oval Greek honey biscuits, each about 7 cm long and 3 cm high, with smooth, softly rounded, slightly flattened egg shapes, piled on a plain white plate. Their surface is a deep, dark chestnut-brown, shiny and wet with glistening amber honey syrup, with a smooth, slightly rough, hand-shaped top. Each one is thickly covered with a generous layer of coarsely crushed walnut pieces and a pinch of ground cinnamon that sticks to the syrup. One biscuit in the front is broken in half to show a moist, dense, crumbly interior, golden brown and soaked darker toward the edges with syrup.
