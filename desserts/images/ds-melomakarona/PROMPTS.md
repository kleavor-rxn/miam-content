## hero.webp
About a dozen oval Greek honey biscuits, each about 6 cm long and 2 cm high, arranged in a loose heap on a plain white plate. They are a deep golden brown, moist and glossy from a honey syrup, with shallow parallel grooves made by a fork across the domed tops. Each one is generously topped with finely chopped light-brown walnuts and a faint dusting of cinnamon that sticks to the syrup. One biscuit in the front is broken in half to show a soft, crumbly, cake-like golden interior darkened at the edges where the syrup has soaked in.
