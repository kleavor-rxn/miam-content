## hero.webp
A generous pile of small Moroccan fried honey pastries, each about 6 cm across, heaped on a plain white plate. Each pastry is a thin sheet of dough cut into parallel strips and folded into a compact rosette, with ridged, crinkled loops and fluted edges like a small flower. They are a deep, glossy mahogany brown, coated in a shiny layer of sticky honey, and thickly sprinkled with pale golden toasted sesame seeds that cling to the honey. A few loose sesame seeds and a thin trail of honey lie on the plate around the pile.
